The company's memory.

New projects ask what could work. Inherited ones ask what is already true.

Kinbase is being built to answer the second question: a store of what the company has decided, and why, that engineers and coding agents read before they change a line. It is the Company product of the three-store Kindex system, developed in this repository under the name Kinbase. This site is the technical documentation; the pitch and the proof condition live at kinbase.tools.
